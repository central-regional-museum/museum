Pick the cards you want. Each one includes its flag, museum number and a code students can scan.

How to print these cards
  1. Choose the items below. Their flags and museum IDs come from the saved collection.
  2. If you change the selection, tap Refresh preview.
  3. To save pictures, choose a size, then Save photo for printing. If you choose several cards, open the downloaded ZIP file to find the pictures inside.
  4. Choose your saved pictures when uploading to your photo service. Select the matching size, full image / no cropping, and check the preview before ordering.
  5. For a school printer, choose Print / save PDF at 100% / Actual size. Scan a test QR card.
Paper printer layouts

The default card is 6 inches wide × 4 inches high. Print at 100% / Actual size. Use the printer’s 4 × 6 paper setting, or print a PDF at actual size and trim. The preview checks whether the text fits.
